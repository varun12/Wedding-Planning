## 2. FAQ

### For Wedding Planners

**What is Shaadi AI and how is it different from Aisle Planner?**
Shaadi AI is the only wedding planning platform built natively for Indian weddings. Aisle Planner was built for single-day Western weddings — it doesn't understand the structure of a multi-event Indian wedding. Shaadi AI comes pre-configured for Mehndi, Haldi, Sangeet, Baraat, Ceremony, and Reception, with AI that understands Indian vendor contracts, cultural norms, and diaspora logistics.

**How does the AI contract review work?**
You upload a vendor contract PDF. Shaadi AI extracts the key terms, flags unusual clauses with a traffic light rating (Green / Yellow / Red), and explains each flag in plain language with a market norm benchmark for Indian wedding vendors. For any flagged clause, you can generate a professional negotiation email in one click.

**How long does contract analysis take?**
Under 60 seconds for contracts up to 25 pages.

**Can I use it with text-based and scanned PDFs?**
Text-based PDFs (exported from Word, Google Docs) upload and extract automatically. Scanned image PDFs require pasting the contract text manually — the app guides you through this with a "Paste Text" tab.

**Is my clients' contract data secure?**
Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Contract PDFs are stored in a private Supabase Storage bucket accessible only to you and the couple for that specific wedding. Contract content is not stored by the AI provider beyond the processing session.

**Can I invite my clients to the dashboard?**
Yes. You invite the couple to a clean status dashboard — they see event progress, budget summary, and vendor confirmations. They don't see the full contract details or your operational view unless you grant access.

**What does it cost?**
Planner — Professional: $199/month (unlimited weddings, all AI features). Planner — Agency: $349/month (multi-planner access, team analytics). Free 3-month trial for beta users.

**What Indian wedding events does it support?**
Pre-configured for: Mehndi, Haldi, Sangeet, Baraat, Wedding Ceremony, Reception. Fully customizable — add, remove, rename, or reorder events. Regional variations (Garba night, Sikh Anand Karaj) supported through the cultural background setup.

### For Couples

**What is Shaadi AI?**
Shaadi AI is the first wedding planning platform built specifically for Indian weddings in North America. It understands that your wedding isn't one event — it's 4-6 events, 20+ vendors, and hundreds of guests. It helps you stay in control without drowning in spreadsheets and WhatsApp threads.

**How does the AI contract review work?**
Upload your vendor contract and Shaadi AI reads it for you — in plain English, at an 8th-grade reading level. It flags anything unusual, explains what it means in practice, and tells you what's standard in the Indian wedding market. If something looks off, it drafts a professional email to the vendor for you.

**Is the AI analysis legally binding or legal advice?**
No. Every summary includes a clear disclaimer: AI-generated summaries are for informational purposes only and are not legal advice. Always verify with a qualified attorney before signing. The feature is designed to help you understand what you're agreeing to — not to replace a lawyer.

**How do I share access with my parents?**
Parents can be given read-only access to budget summaries and event status. They don't see vendor contracts or planning details. Access is controlled by you or your planner.

**What if my planner already uses Shaadi AI?**
Your planner invites you to the couple dashboard. You get a clean view of your wedding — event status, budget summary, vendor confirmations, and guest counts — without needing to manage the operational details yourself.

## 3. Onboarding Guides

### Planner Onboarding Guide (outline)

**Step 1 — Account setup (5 min)**
- Sign up, select Planner role
- Complete style profile: paste 3–5 past vendor emails to train AI tone matching

**Step 2 — Create your first wedding (10 min)**
- Enter couple names, wedding date, total budget
- Complete cultural background interview — platform generates custom event lineup
- Invite couple to their dashboard

**Step 3 — Upload your first contract (5 min)**
- Navigate to Contracts
- Upload a PDF or paste contract text
- Enter vendor name and category
- Review AI summary, flags, and obligations

**Step 4 — Explore the platform (15 min)**
- Add vendors and track status
- Review pre-suggested budget allocations across events
- Import guest list (paste from any format)

**Estimated onboarding time: 30–45 minutes**

### Couple Onboarding Guide (outline)

**Step 1 — Accept planner invite or sign up directly**

**Step 2 — Review your wedding structure**
- Confirm event lineup and dates

- Start with your venue or photographer contract
- Review the summary and flags together as a couple

- Invite parents to read-only budget view
