# Capstone Video Script — Shaadi AI
**Format:** 3 slides + live demo | **Total runtime:** 4:00  
**Delivery:** Screen recording with voiceover narration

---

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- Record slides and demo as separate takes, edit together
- Browser zoom: 125% before recording so text is readable
- Use the mock contract in `capstone_demo_contract.md` — paste text mode for reliability
- Speak at a relaxed pace — 4 minutes feels short but rushed narration is hard to follow
- No background music needed; clear narration is more professional

## SLIDE 1 — THE PROBLEM
**[On screen: Full-bleed slide with comparison table — Western vs. Indian wedding]**  
**Runtime: 0:00–0:30**

> "Priya is a wedding planner in Toronto. She manages 15 South Asian weddings a year — each one spanning 5 to 6 events, 300 guests, and $250,000 in vendor contracts.
>
> Every tool she has was built for a single-day Western wedding. The Knot. Aisle Planner. Google Sheets. None of them understand that an Indian wedding is not one event — it's six.
>
> So she does what every Indian wedding planner does. She juggles WhatsApp threads, color-coded spreadsheets, and email chains — manually, for every wedding, every year."

## DEMO — AI CONTRACT INTELLIGENCE
**[On screen: Shaadi AI app, live in browser]**  
**Runtime: 1:00–3:30**

### Scene 1 — Dashboard (0:10)
*[Show the dashboard with a wedding already set up — 5 events visible: Mehndi, Haldi, Sangeet, Ceremony, Reception]*

> "This is Priya's dashboard inside Shaadi AI. She's managing Neha and Arjun's wedding — five events, 280 guests, June 2027.
>
> Her photography vendor just sent over a contract."

---

### Scene 2 — Navigate to Contracts and Upload (0:20)
*[Click to Contracts page. Click "Analyze Contract." Select "Paste Text" tab. Paste the mock contract text. Enter vendor name: Moments by Kapoor Photography. Category: Photography. Click Analyze.]*

> "She goes to the Contracts page and pastes in the contract text. Vendor is Moments by Kapoor Photography — photography for all five events.
>
> She hits Analyze."

---

### Scene 3 — Analysis Returns (0:40)
*[Wait for the analysis to load. Show the plain-language summary first — scroll through payment schedule, cancellation policy, what's included.]*

> "In under 60 seconds, Shaadi AI returns a plain-language summary of every clause in the contract — payment schedule, cancellation terms, what's included, what's not.
>
> Written at a reading level anyone can understand. No legal background required."

*[Scroll down to the traffic light flags.]*

> "And here's where it gets powerful. Every clause is rated — green, yellow, or red.
>
> Yellow flag on the cancellation terms — 75% of the total contract value owed with less than 90 days' notice. Shaadi AI flags this as above the industry norm for Indian wedding photography in North America."

*[Point to the RED flag — force majeure.]*

> "And a red flag. No force majeure clause. If the photographer gets sick or has an emergency on the wedding day — there is no protection in this contract for Neha and Arjun. Shaadi AI caught it. The couple wouldn't have."

---

### Scene 4 — Draft Response (0:40)
*[Click "Draft Response" on the red flag.]*

> "Priya clicks Draft Response on the red flag.
>
> Shaadi AI generates a professional negotiation email — addressed to the vendor, naming the specific clause, referencing what's standard in the market, and proposing alternative language."

*[Show the drafted email on screen. Scroll through it slowly.]*

> "She reviews it, makes one small edit, and copies it to her email client. What would have taken 30 minutes of research and drafting — done in one click."

---

### Scene 5 — Obligation Tracker (0:40)
*[Navigate to the obligation tracker or show it populated below the contract summary.]*

> "And when the contract is signed, Shaadi AI extracts every time-bound obligation automatically.
>
> Payment due 60 days out. Final payment on the day of the first event. Confirmation call two weeks before.
>
> All of it tracked. All of it with automated reminders. Nothing falls through the cracks."
