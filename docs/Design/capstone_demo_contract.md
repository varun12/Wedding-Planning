PHOTOGRAPHY SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Photography Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of May 1, 2026, between Moments by Kapoor Photography ("Photographer") and Neha Sharma & Arjun Patel ("Clients").

---

**1. EVENT DETAILS**

The Photographer agrees to provide photography coverage for the following events:

- Mehndi Ceremony — June 12, 2027 | The Mississauga Convention Centre, Mississauga, ON
- Haldi Ceremony — June 13, 2027 | Private Residence, Oakville, ON
- Sangeet Night — June 13, 2027 | The Mississauga Convention Centre, Mississauga, ON
- Wedding Ceremony & Reception — June 14, 2027 | The Venetian Banquet Hall, Vaughan, ON

Total contracted hours: Up to 14 hours across all events.

---

**2. SERVICES INCLUDED**

The following services are included under this Agreement:

- Two (2) professional photographers for all contracted events
- Online digital gallery delivered within 8–10 weeks of the final event
- Up to 800 edited digital images across all events
- One (1) complimentary engagement session (up to 2 hours)
- Private online gallery hosted for 12 months

---

**3. SERVICES NOT INCLUDED**

The following are not included and may be arranged at additional cost:

- Wedding albums or printed materials of any kind
- Drone or aerial photography
- Videography or video editing services
- Same-day edits or previews
- Rush delivery of gallery (available at $500 surcharge)
- Additional photographers beyond the two (2) included

---

**4. PAYMENT SCHEDULE**

The total contracted fee is $8,500 CAD.

Payment shall be made as follows:

- Retainer (non-refundable): $4,250 CAD (50%) — due upon signing
- Second payment: $2,125 CAD (25%) — due 60 days prior to the first event
- Final payment: $2,125 CAD (25%) — due on or before the day of the first contracted event

All payments are due by the dates specified. A late fee of $150 will be applied for payments received more than 5 days after the due date. Payments may be made by e-transfer to payments@momentsbykапoor.com.

---

**5. CANCELLATION POLICY**

**By the Client:**

All retainer payments are non-refundable under any circumstances.

If the Client cancels this Agreement:
- More than 180 days before the first event: Retainer forfeited; no additional fees
- Between 90 and 180 days before the first event: 60% of total contract value due
- Less than 90 days before the first event: 75% of total contract value due
- Less than 30 days before the first event: 100% of total contract value due

**By the Photographer:**

In the event the Photographer must cancel due to unforeseen personal circumstances, the Photographer will make reasonable efforts to refer a replacement photographer of comparable skill. If no replacement is arranged, the Photographer will refund all payments received to date within 30 days.

---

**6. OVERTIME**

Coverage beyond the contracted hours will be billed at $250 CAD per hour, per photographer, in 30-minute increments. Overtime charges are due within 14 days of the final event.

---

**7. EXCLUSIVITY**

The Photographer shall serve as the exclusive professional photographer at all contracted events. The Clients agree not to hire any other professional photographers for the same events without prior written consent from the Photographer. Guests and family members using personal devices are permitted.

---

**8. DELIVERY TIMELINE**

The edited gallery will be delivered within 8–10 weeks of the final contracted event. Delays caused by circumstances outside the Photographer's control (including illness, equipment failure, or travel disruption) may extend the delivery timeline without penalty to the Photographer.

---

**9. COPYRIGHT AND USAGE**

The Photographer retains full copyright to all images produced under this Agreement. The Clients are granted a personal, non-commercial license to print and share images for personal use. Images may not be sold, licensed, or used for commercial purposes without the written consent of the Photographer. The Photographer reserves the right to use images for portfolio, website, and social media promotion unless the Clients provide written objection within 14 days of signing.

---

**10. LIABILITY**

The Photographer's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total contract value paid by the Clients. The Photographer is not liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages of any kind, including emotional distress.

In the event of equipment failure, digital file corruption, or other technical failure, the Photographer's sole obligation is to provide a partial refund proportional to the coverage lost. No refunds will be issued for coverage delivered and deemed unsatisfactory on subjective grounds.

---

**11. GOVERNING LAW**

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario. Any disputes arising from this Agreement shall be resolved through binding arbitration in the City of Toronto.

---

**12. ENTIRE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties. No verbal representations made prior to signing shall be considered binding. Amendments must be made in writing and signed by both parties.

---

Signed:

Client 1: Neha Sharma  
Client 2: Arjun Patel  
Photographer: Rahul Kapoor, Moments by Kapoor Photography  
Date: May 1, 2026

## Expected AI Output

When this contract is analyzed in Shaadi AI, it should produce:

**Risk Score: Medium**

**RED flag:** No force majeure clause — Section 8 mentions delays due to illness and equipment failure but only in the context of delivery timelines, not event cancellation. There is no protection for the couple if the photographer is unable to attend due to illness, accident, or emergency on the wedding day. Standard Indian wedding photography contracts in Canada include a force majeure clause covering both parties.

**YELLOW flag:** Cancellation terms heavily favor the vendor — 75% of total contract value ($6,375 CAD) is owed with less than 90 days' notice. Industry standard for Indian wedding photography in North America is 50–60% within 90 days. The asymmetry between client cancellation penalties (up to 100%) and photographer cancellation terms (refund only, no compensation) is notable.

**GREEN flags:** Payment schedule (standard 50/25/25 split), overtime rate ($250/hour — within normal range), delivery timeline (8–10 weeks — standard for multi-event coverage), exclusivity clause (standard).
